Check the fan current before using C828

A common 2SC828 datasheet lists 50 mA as the absolute maximum continuous collector current. A small fan can draw more current at startup than its normal running current. Read the fan label or measure its current first. If its startup/running current is near or above the transistor's safe limit, do not use C828 for that fan; substitute a suitably rated higher-current NPN transistor and verify its pinout. Never test by connecting the fan directly to an ESP32 pin.

03 · ASSEMBLY NOTES

Before applying power

LM35 orientation

For the TI LP TO-92 package, the datasheet shows the pinout from the bottom view: pin 1 +Vs, pin 2 VOUT, pin 3 GND. Confirm the package/view before wiring; other LM35 packages have different pin arrangements.

I²C voltage levels

Use a bidirectional I²C level shifter when the LCD backpack is powered at 5V. ESP32-C3 pins are 3.3V-domain; do not let a 5V pull-up reach SDA/SCL GPIOs. If your LCD/backpack is confirmed 3.3V-compatible, the level shifter may be unnecessary.

C828 lead arrangement

The drawing names the transistor terminals functionally as B, C and E. A common ST 2SC828 TO-92 datasheet lists pins 1=E, 2=C, 3=B, but it also allows different pin configurations. Check the exact part's datasheet before inserting it.

Power the board once

Feed regulated 5V into the board's marked 5V pin or power it by USB-C; do not connect both sources together unless the exact board's power circuit confirms that is safe. Never apply 5V to a GPIO or the 3V3 pin.

Fan type and PWM

Check the fan label and current, including startup current. Some 2-wire brushless fans do not respond smoothly to low-side PWM and may need a different control method or a fan designed for PWM control.

Power supply grounds

Keep the +5V and +9V rails separate. Only connect their negative (GND) terminals together. Do not connect +9V to the ESP32, LM35 output, LCD logic pins or GPIOs.

  1. With power disconnected, check the LM35, transistor and diode orientations.
  2. Power the ESP32/LCD/sensor from regulated 5V first and confirm the LCD and temperature reading.
  3. Only connect the fan supply after confirming the fan current and choosing a transistor with adequate rating.
